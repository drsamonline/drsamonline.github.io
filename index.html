About

The person behind the builds

Dr. Sohil Momin, BHMS is a homeopathic physician and a builder of open-source, offline-first software for medicine, learning and play.

He writes software the way he practises: it should work when the network doesn't, respect the person using it, and need no account to be useful. That belief runs through everything here — single-file apps, private tools that keep your data on your device, and a modern re-telling of the classical homeopathic materia medica.

Alongside medicine he ships games, engines, a compiler, a browser experiment and everyday utilities — because building is how ideas get tested. Almost everything is free to explore, and much of it is free to use.
